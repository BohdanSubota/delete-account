Delete Your OUT! Account

This page explains how to request permanent deletion of your OUT! account and the personal data associated with it.

App: OUT!

Developer: OUT Technologies

Package: com.imout

How to request account deletion

You can request deletion in either of these ways:

  • In the app: Open OUT! → Profile → “Request to Delete Account” and confirm.
  • By email (no app required): Send a message to info@out-technologies.com with the subject “Delete OUT! Account” and include the phone number linked to your account.

What we delete

When your request is processed, we delete:

  • Your account and profile details (name, phone number, avatar)
  • Location history and shared trip / event data linked to your account
  • Messages, reactions, and other content associated with your account
  • Device and usage data tied to your account

What may be retained

We may retain limited information only when required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or regulatory reasons, and only for as long as necessary.

Processing time

We aim to complete account and data deletion within 30 days after verifying your request.
